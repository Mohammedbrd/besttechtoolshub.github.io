Editor's Verdict — WinZip: Why Are People Still Paying For This?

WinZip is the most unnecessary paid software in existence. Windows 11 and macOS both have built-in ZIP support. For advanced formats (RAR, 7z, TAR), 7-Zip is completely free, open-source, and faster than WinZip. WinZip costs $39.99/year for functionality that's been free for 20 years.

The only reason WinZip still exists is brand recognition from the 1990s and aggressive pre-installation on new PCs. Our honest advice: uninstall WinZip immediately, download 7-Zip (7-zip.org), and never think about file compression again. If you need encryption, 7-Zip supports AES-256. If you need cloud integration, use the built-in tools in Windows/Google Drive. There is literally no reason to pay for WinZip in 2026.
