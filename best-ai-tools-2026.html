How to Choose the Right AI Tools for You

  • Count your subscriptions first. If you pay for 2+ AI tools, an all-in-one platform will almost certainly save you money.
  • Match the tool to the task. A writer doesn't need a video generator; a creator doesn't need a PDF suite. Start with what you'll actually use.
  • Check commercial rights. If you create for clients or business, confirm the license allows commercial use of generated content.
  • Start free, upgrade when needed. Most platforms offer free tiers or trials. Test before you commit.
  • Watch for limits. "Unlimited" plans often have hidden caps on the most powerful models. Read the fine print.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI tool is best overall in 2026?

For most people, an all-in-one platform like Lorka AI is the best overall choice because it gives you access to every major model for one price. If you only need one specific function (just chat, or just images), a dedicated tool may be simpler.

Are free AI tools good enough?

Free tiers are great for casual use and learning. But if you use AI for work or content creation, paid tiers offer better models, higher limits, faster speeds, and commercial rights — and usually pay for themselves quickly.

Can I use AI-generated content commercially?

It depends on the platform's license. Most major tools allow commercial use on paid plans, but always check the terms. All-in-one platforms like Lorka include a commercial license on their Max plan.

How much should I spend on AI tools per month?

Most casual users spend $0–$20/month. Professionals and creators often spend $20–$50. If you're paying more than that, you're probably over-subscribed — consolidating into one platform can cut that bill significantly.
