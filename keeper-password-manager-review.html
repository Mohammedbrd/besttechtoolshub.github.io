What Is Keeper?

Founded in 2011 and based in Chicago, Keeper Security builds encrypted password-management and cybersecurity tools. Its flagship product — the Keeper password manager — stores your passwords, passkeys, credit cards, identity info, and private files in an encrypted vault that syncs across every device you own. Because Keeper uses zero-knowledge encryption, your master password and vault data are never stored on Keeper's servers in readable form.

Keeper is available on Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, Linux, and every major browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge), plus a web vault.

Key Features

🔐 Zero-Knowledge AES-256 Bank-grade encryption; only you hold the decryption key.
🔑 Passkey Support Save and autofill passkeys alongside passwords.
📱 Unlimited Devices Sync your vault across phone, laptop, tablet, and desktop.
🛡️ Built-In TOTP Generate 2FA codes inside Keeper — no separate authenticator app needed.
👥 Secure Sharing Share individual logins or whole folders with permissions.
🚨 Emergency Access Grant a trusted contact vault access if something happens to you.
💬 KeeperChat Encrypted messaging app included with paid plans.
🌑 BreachWatch (add-on) Dark-web monitoring alerts you if your credentials leak.

How Does It Compare?

Compared with 1Password, Keeper is roughly similar in price but includes encrypted messaging and a more generous family storage allowance. Compared with Bitwarden (which has a genuinely useful free tier), Keeper's free plan is far more restricted — but Keeper adds polished apps, emergency access, and built-in TOTP on its entry paid plan. If you want a free-forever option, Bitwarden wins. If you want a polished, business-grade vault with family sharing, Keeper is excellent.

Who Should Use Keeper?

  • ✅ Anyone who wants the strongest security with the least hassle
  • ✅ Families who need separate vaults with controlled sharing
  • ✅ Users on Linux or less-common platforms
  • ✅ People who want 2FA codes and encrypted chat in one app
  • ❌ Users who need a fully-featured free plan (try Bitwarden instead)
  • ❌ Anyone who wants a money-back guarantee (use the 30-day trial)

Final Verdict

Keeper is one of the most secure, fully-featured password managers you can buy in 2026. Its zero-knowledge architecture, cross-platform support, built-in TOTP, and strong family plan make it an easy recommendation — especially if you've had it with reused passwords and want one vault that follows you everywhere. Just use the 30-day free trial to decide before you pay, since there's no refund window afterward.
