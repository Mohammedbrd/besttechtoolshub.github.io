What Is Keeper?

Founded in 2011 and based in Chicago, Keeper Security is a SOC 2 certified, zero-knowledge password manager. Zero-knowledge means even Keeper's own employees cannot see your data — everything is encrypted on your device before it ever reaches their servers. Available on Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, and all major browsers, Keeper syncs your vault across unlimited devices.
